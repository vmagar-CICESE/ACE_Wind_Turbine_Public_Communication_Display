# Challenge 5: ACE Public Communication Display

**Deadline:** October 28th, 2026

**Posted by:** [Charlie Plumley](https://community.wedowind.ch/members/28777183) ⭐️

> Create an engaging public display that uses live data from the ACE wind turbine to help people in Lawrence Weston understand what the turbine is doing and the value it creates for their community.

## Background

[Ambition Community Energy (ACE)](https://ambitioncommunityenergy.org/) is a community interest company established by residents of Lawrence Weston, Bristol, UK, through their resident-led, open-membership regeneration charity, [Ambition Lawrence Weston (ALW)](https://www.ambitionlw.org/).

ACE was created to build and operate a 4.2 MW wind turbine, with the aim of generating income to support ALW's work tackling deprivation and poverty in Lawrence Weston. Please see the ACE and ALW websites for more information.

ACE is making data from its wind turbine openly available under a [CC-BY-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode) licence to help:

1. Increase the visibility of the ACE wind turbine, particularly within the community that owns it.
2. Encourage local students and others to take an interest in renewable energy.
3. Provide live data access to universities, researchers and other interested users, including for the development of new techniques such as short-term wind-power forecasting.

In addition to the historical datasets stored on [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22662372), a live [dashboard](https://ace-api.duckdns.org/dashboard/) and [API](https://ace-api.duckdns.org/docs) provide access to the most recent data.

## The Challenge

ACE would like to create a public-facing display for the local community centre that raises awareness of the wind turbine and communicates the value it provides to the Lawrence Weston community.

The challenge is to create a **dashboard, interactive display, digital twin, data artwork, or other creative visual experience** that can be shown on a screen in the community centre.

The solution should use live data from the ACE wind turbine, available through the [API](https://ace-api.duckdns.org/docs), to help members of the public understand the turbine, what it is doing, and the value it creates for their community.

Solutions should be designed primarily for a **non-technical public audience**. Participants are encouraged to think creatively about how live wind turbine data can be made engaging, accessible and meaningful.

The winning solution will be publicly displayed and made accessible alongside, or potentially as a replacement for, the current integrated [dashboard](https://ace-api.duckdns.org/dashboard/), subject to technical review.

Solutions should be released under an appropriate open licence and provided with everything needed for others to reproduce and adapt them.

## Submissions and Presentation

To enter the challenge, solutions should be submitted in the "[Solutions](https://community.wedowind.ch/posts/open-data-exploration-solutions-107654528)" task by adding a comment with a URL, brief description and screenshot. You can also add your solution to the [GitHub page](https://wedowind.github.io/WeDoWind-ODE-ACE-Challenge/), including source code via a pull request to the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/WeDoWind/WeDoWind-ODE-ACE-Challenge).

You are welcome to provide positive, constructive feedback to others during this process, and you can work together on a team solution.

On **Wednesday 28 October** there will be a presentation call, where each participant or team will have some time to share and pitch their creation, after which there will be a public vote open to everyone attending the meeting. **The solution receiving the most votes will be the Overall Winner.**

Two additional prizes will be presented by Charlie Plumley:
- A **Judge's Award**, taking into account the criteria below
- A **Community Contribution Award**, recognising positive engagement with the challenge and support for others

### What We're Looking For

There is no prescribed format for the solution, and participants are encouraged to explore creative approaches. Submissions might consider:

- **Public accessibility:** Is the display easy to understand and engaging for people with little or no technical knowledge of wind energy?
- **Effective use of live data:** Does the solution make meaningful use of the live ACE wind turbine data and help communicate what the turbine is doing?
- **Community relevance:** Does it help communicate the value and significance of the turbine to Lawrence Weston and its residents?
- **Creativity and engagement:** Does the solution present the data in an interesting, memorable or innovative way?
- **Practicality and robustness:** Is the solution suitable for continuous or regular display on a screen in a community setting, with minimal intervention?

Participants do not need to address all of these aspects equally. A simple idea that communicates effectively may be just as valuable as a more technically complex solution, and technical sophistication is not a judging criterion in itself.

## Minimum Requirements

- Uses live data from the ACE API
- Is suitable for display on a screen
- Is designed primarily for a non-technical public audience
- Is shared under an appropriate open licence with enough information to reproduce it
