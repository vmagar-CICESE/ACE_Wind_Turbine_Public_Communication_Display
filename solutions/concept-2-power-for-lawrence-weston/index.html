About this turbine

Ambition Community Energy (ACE) is a community interest company set up by residents of Lawrence Weston, Bristol, through their resident-led charity Ambition Lawrence Weston (ALW), to build and operate this 4.2 MW turbine and help tackle poverty in the area.
