# Licence scope and third-party content

The [MIT licence](LICENSE) covers the original source code and documentation
in this repository — the `solutions/` concepts and the helper scripts and
notes in `materials/`. Each concept folder also carries its own copy of the
licence.

Content that is **not** covered by the MIT licence:

- **ACE wind turbine data** shown by the displays and fetched live from
  `https://ace-api.duckdns.org`. It is published by Ambition Community Energy
  (ACE) under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) —
  dataset DOI [10.5281/zenodo.22662372](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22662372).
- `materials/api-reference/` and `materials/api-samples/` — verbatim
  responses from the public ACE API, kept for reference (ACE data, CC BY 4.0).
- `materials/challenge-repo-guides/` — reference copies of documents from the
  [WeDoWind ODE ACE Challenge repository](https://github.com/WeDoWind/WeDoWind-ODE-ACE-Challenge),
  which keep the licence terms of that repository.
- `instructions/` — the challenge brief, reproduced for context; copyright of
  its authors.
